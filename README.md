A 100-word monologue for [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) — a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the deflections, and the sentences that trail off, never in a plain statement of it. He still has a scheme: the salt tax, the river gates, every mill in Valdris.

## The monologue

> Come in. Mind the third step. It still bites. I had it enchanted in '87, back when traps sang.
>
> You want the crown? Take it. No — sit. The tea's cold, the ravens stopped answering my letters, which is… fine.
>
> Tonight the river gates open at moonrise. My barges — black sails, the ones your grandfather burned — carry the salt tax upriver. By dawn every mill in Valdris owes me. Not blood. Rent.
>
> Don't look so shocked. I kept the receipts. Forty years, in the cellar, next to the —
>
> …where was I? Right. Kneel, or don't. The floor's cold either way.

103 words, 550 characters.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes entry.txt to $ENTRY_OUTPUT_DIR (default /output)
```

Stdlib only, no network. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
